## Artwork: license verification pending

**The MPL grant above does not cover the bundled artwork.** This includes
images and other visual assets in `Sources/Avatar/Avatar.xcassets/`, artwork
masters in `Scripts/Artwork/`, screenshots, rendered avatars, and images
embedded in documentation. An asset-specific license may state otherwise.

The original graphics package and its license have not been identified.
The maintainer recalls that commercial use was allowed, but the original
terms and redistribution permissions have not been verified. Later edits or
regeneration do not establish the rights to redistribute retained or derived
artwork. For example, [the glasses documentation](Docs/glasses-frame-colors.md)
records original bitmap artwork still used by several styles.

This repository therefore makes no blanket license grant for its artwork.
Licensing the rendering and generation code does not resolve rights in source
images or resulting artwork. Do not treat the code license as permission to
redistribute the complete asset collection or exported avatar images.

To resolve this, identify the source and applicable terms for each retained
third-party asset and preserve required notices, or replace it with artwork
whose provenance and redistribution permissions are documented. Individually
verified assets can then receive explicit license notices.
